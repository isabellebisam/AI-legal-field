Artificial Intelligence in Digital Law and Intellectual Property

An exploration of how AI is transforming legal practice, with a focus on digital rights, copyright enforcement, and algorithmic decision-making.

About This Project

This website examines the intersection of artificial intelligence and legal practice, particularly within the domains of digital law and intellectual property. As AI systems increasingly automate legal processes—from copyright enforcement to risk assessment—understanding their capabilities, limitations, and implications becomes essential for legal professionals, policymakers, and creators.

Why This Matters

AI-driven systems are reshaping how legal decisions are made. Automated copyright enforcement tools process millions of claims daily. Algorithmic risk assessments influence bail and sentencing. AI-generated content challenges traditional notions of authorship and ownership. These developments raise critical questions about transparency, accountability, bias, and access to justice.
